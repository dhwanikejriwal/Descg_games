What is GDP?

Gross Domestic Product (GDP) — or GSDP for our state — is the total monetary value of all final goods and services produced within Chhattisgarh in a single financial year. It is the master scorecard of our state's economic size, wealth, and progress.

How GSDP is Calculated:
🌾🏭 All Goods Produced Agriculture, Minerals, Bhilai Steel, Power & Manufacturing
+
💻🏥 Every Service Provided Healthcare, Transport, IT, Banking & Education
